Written from the heart, for anyone who has loved and lost

For years, I’ve carried a story in my heart. One More Day is that story — about life, love, loss… and finding yourself again when you never thought you could.

Every page has been written from the heart. When it’s finally in your hands, I hope it reminds you to cherish each moment — and never give up on hope.

— from the announcement to readers, July 2026

Words that found 219,000 hearts

  • Letting go of something precious may be as simple as saying: ‘Thank you for the journey.’

The heart behind the page

One More Day began as a place to put words to the things we all feel and rarely say. What started as a single page about love, loss, and healing has grown into a daily gathering of more than 219,000 people. The book carries that same heart — written by the same hands, for the same reasons. One More Day is the debut novel of Chris Post.
